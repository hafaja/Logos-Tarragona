    Areas of work

    We work for people and for the organisations that support them.

    Social projects

    Projects and actions focused on support, participation, autonomy and inclusion of diverse people and groups.

    Technical services

    Project design, studies, technical support, monitoring, evaluation and team reinforcement for public bodies and organisations.

    Our activities

    • Training and information for groups at risk of social exclusion.
    • Career guidance, promotion and job placement.
    • Development and management of programmes, studies and actions for other organisations, institutions or companies.
    • Employment inclusion through workshops and service companies.
    • Social consultancy and digital divide.

    Our support model

    Comprehensive Inclusion Service: we support the whole person, because lack of income is often not the only barrier to inclusion.

    1
    Welcome

    Individual and family: we listen to and understand each person's situation.

    2
    Support

    A tailored social and employment pathway with an individual work plan.

    3
    Advice and support

    Emotional, legal and digital support, plus Arrela't first-reception training.

    Logos Employment

    Our team has extensive experience running employment programmes. We see employment as both a means and a goal.

    Programmes we run

    • Integrated employment services
    • Guidance, information and job-search actions
    • Minimum income inclusion pathways
    • Job placement agencies
    • Active inclusion measures
    • Guidance programmes

    Who we work with

    People at risk of exclusion, prioritising women and young people:

    • Long-term unemployed people
    • Unemployed people in age groups facing more barriers
    • Young people with little experience or training, or looking for their first job
    • Trained young people lacking key skills
    • People with very specific professional profiles and resistance to change

    Skills and services

    Tap each service to see the details.

    Consultancy and technical assistance

    Support and advice for social projects, strategic consultancy and local project design, technical assistance and management, evaluation and monitoring, promotion of social responsibility, gender equality plans and ISO 9001 quality systems for organisations.

    Advice and management for organisations

    Since 2021 we have supported more than 70 organisations: general procedures, grants and online dealings with the administration, project technical assistance and building agreements and external relations.

    Social studies

    Social event management, campaign coordination, root-cause studies, social and anthropological research, and HR support.

    Training

    Training needs analysis, design, management and delivery of training, with quality control under ISO 9001:2015.

    Social enterprises

    Together with Fundació Intress, Logos created the social enterprise Intress Creix, and helped set up Iniciativa Aptos Empresa d'Inserció. We carry out market studies and reports for social enterprises and sheltered employment centres.

    Why be socially responsible

    Working with social enterprises and social projects adds value to a company and to the community.

    • Adds value and a positive reputation locally
    • Improves image and attracts and retains customers
    • Increases social influence and reduces conflict
    • Makes the economic and industrial model sustainable
    • Brings innovation and creativity to processes and services
    • Improves quality across the whole supply chain
    • Helps create stable, quality jobs
    • Encourages responsible consumption
    • Improves the local environment
    • Brings savings and economic benefits
