    Transparency

    Transparency and accountability

    At Logos we work for responsible, ethical and effective management. We make the relevant institutional and financial documents about our work publicly available.

    Our commitment to transparency

    Transparency is a core principle of how Logos works. We see accountability as a natural part of our commitment to people, public administrations, organisations and the local community.

    That is why we publish the relevant financial and institutional documents in an accessible way, aiming to offer clear, useful and easy-to-consult information.

    Logos Tarragona

    • Non-profit associationAn organisation focused on social, training and inclusion projects.
    • Founded in 2015Born from needs identified in the area, with the aim of offering useful, close-at-hand responses.
    • Partnership workWorks with public bodies, organisations and other partners to develop initiatives with local impact.
    • Own and joint projectsRuns its own initiatives and also provides technical and management support to programmes of other organisations and institutions.
